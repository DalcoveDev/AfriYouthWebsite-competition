## Project Overview

The project focuses on designing and developing a modern, responsive, and user-friendly website for AfriYouth Innovation Labs, communicating its identity, work, programs, services, and opportunities.

## Objectives

- Present AfriYouth Innovation Labs clearly.
- Help young people discover programs and opportunities.
- Communicate services and areas of work to organizations.
- Support potential partners in exploring collaboration.
- Deliver an accessible, responsive, and functional website.
